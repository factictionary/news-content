02 — Usage

How We Use Information

Factictionary (the developer of SpoofDaily) does not receive, process, or use any personal data from users. We do not operate a backend server that receives user data.

The only data processing that occurs is by Google AdMob, solely for the purpose of serving and measuring advertisements within the app. We do not use any data to build user profiles, make automated decisions about users, or share data with any party other than Google AdMob as described in Section 3.

Plain language: We get nothing. AdMob gets your Advertising ID to show you relevant ads. That's the full extent of it.
03 — Third Parties

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

SpoofDaily uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google LLC and is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

AdMob may use your device's Advertising Identifier (Android Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) to serve personalised advertisements. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time — see Section 5.

GitHub (Content Delivery)

The app's daily news feed is fetched from a GitHub repository via HTTPS. This is a standard HTTP request; GitHub may log the request IP address in accordance with their own privacy policy. No personal information is sent in this request.

GitHub Privacy Statement:
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

No other third-party SDKs

SpoofDaily does not include any analytics SDKs (Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), social media SDKs, crash reporting services, or any other third-party data collection tools beyond those listed above.

04 — Security

Data Storage & Security

Encrypted content

The daily news feed fetched from our servers is encrypted using AES-256-CBC with a daily rotating key. The cached content stored on your device is stored in your app's private SharedPreferences storage, which is only accessible to SpoofDaily.

Transmission security

All network communication by SpoofDaily uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher). Plain HTTP requests are not permitted by the app's network configuration.

No central server

Factictionary does not operate a user-facing backend server. There is no database containing user accounts, preferences, or behaviour data held by us. Your data is only on your device and on Google's AdMob servers.

05 — Your Rights

Your Rights & Choices

Opt out of personalised ads

You can opt out of interest-based advertising at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Google Ad Settings: adssettings.google.com

When you opt out, you will still see ads in SpoofDaily — they will simply be less personalised.

Reset your Advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → (reset available per-app)

Delete locally stored data

You can delete all locally stored app data (cached feed, preferences, bookmarks) by:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → SpoofDaily → Storage → Clear Data
  • iOS: Delete and reinstall the app

Request deletion of AdMob data

To request deletion of data held by Google AdMob, please visit: myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy

GDPR (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including the right to access, rectify, erase, and port your data. Since Factictionary holds no personal data, requests relating to AdMob data should be directed to Google. For any other privacy requests, contact us at the address in Section 8.

CCPA (California Users)

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. SpoofDaily does not sell personal information. The sharing of Advertising ID data with Google AdMob constitutes "sharing" under CCPA for cross-context behavioural advertising purposes. You may opt out via the ad settings described above.

06 — Children

Children's Privacy

SpoofDaily is not directed at children under the age of 13 and is not designed or intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Our content rating is Everyone / All Ages because the satire content is clean and family-friendly. However, the advertising within the app is standard Google AdMob advertising not configured under COPPA child-directed treatment.

If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us at factictionary@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.
